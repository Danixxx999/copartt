# Imágenes ilustrativas de copart

Generadas con el **tool integrado image_gen**, sin CLI ni claves API. No son fotos de vehículos reales a la venta. No se copiaron fotografías ni banners de Copart. Los recursos originales de vendedores conservan prioridad; la sustitución visual solo se aplica a los antiguos placeholders `/media/car/automobile.svg` y `/media/car/suv.svg`.

## Archivos guardados en el proyecto

- `public/img/copart/hero-suv-lineup.jpg` — banner de portada; fuente PNG conservada en la misma carpeta.
- `public/img/copart/demo-sedan.jpg` — imagen ilustrativa para automóviles demo; fuente PNG conservada.
- `public/img/copart/demo-suv.jpg` — imagen ilustrativa para SUVs demo; fuente PNG conservada.

Las versiones JPG son exportaciones de las imágenes PNG generadas. Se muestran etiquetas de imagen ilustrativa en las tarjetas y la ficha; no se agregan vistas fotográficas ficticias a la galería. Si el vendedor ha cargado fotos, se conserva su galería completa.

## Prompts finales

### hero-suv-lineup

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide photographic automotive website hero background for copart. Generate a premium realistic cutout-style lineup of four unbranded luxury used SUVs, black crossover far left, silver SUV second, deep charcoal SUV third, large pearl white SUV foreground far right, all three-quarter front views facing slightly left, evenly parked on an almost black deep petrol ground with realistic soft tire shadows. Wide landscape composition 1536x1024 or widest available: all four vehicles occupy the bottom right 65 percent of the image, leaving the left third clean empty dark petrol for separately rendered HTML text. Upper half dark petrol studio atmosphere. On the far right background add a broad diagonal slash in bright sky blue, pale silver grey beyond. Professional automotive catalog photography, physically accurate wheels grilles reflections windows, neutral daylight, crisp clean cars, no accident damage. No lettering, no logos, no text, no watermarks, no UI. This is illustrative banner artwork and not a specific vehicle listing.

### demo-sedan

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: clearly illustrative demo inventory photograph for an academic used-vehicle auction website. Create a highly realistic used dark graphite grey European compact four-door passenger car, model inspired by a mid-2010s compact hatchback but no manufacturer badge or identifiable logo. Three quarter front-left exterior photo, car facing lower left, full vehicle visible with space around all wheels, normal condition with light wear and minor fender scratch, inside a clean warehouse with silver corrugated garage doors behind and worn concrete floor, natural overcast light, realistic windshield reflection, slightly elevated documentary camera like an auction listing photo. Landscape 4:3 composition. No humans, no lettering, no number plate text, no watermarks, no UI. Visually this must be a photograph, not vector art, not cartoon. No branded signs. This will be labeled Imagen ilustrativa in the app.
